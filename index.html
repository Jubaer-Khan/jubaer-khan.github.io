About

Embedded software is most of my working life: C and C++ on RTOS and Embedded Linux targets, built for automotive and industrial clients at BJIT, where I have been since 2022 and now work as a Senior Software Engineer.

Much of it comes down to communication — CAN 2.0B and SAE J1939 in particular — and to the less glamorous side of safety-critical development: design review, MISRA compliance, and getting a Yocto image to behave on real hardware. The work is not only embedded, though. Qt and .NET desktop applications take up a good share of it, as does migrating an ageing VB6 system to a web-based C#/.NET one. Lately I have been building agent-based tooling.

My research interests began during my degree at North South University and have stayed since. I co-authored XCR-Net, a deep-learning framework for detecting COVID-19 and pneumonia from chest X-rays, published in IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics. Medical imaging and biomedical text extraction both still hold my attention.
